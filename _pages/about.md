I am a dual-degree student in Electronics and Electrical Communication Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, graduating in 2027. My research sits at the intersection of **Computer Architecture**, **Hardware Security**, and **Systems**. I am interested in exploring **Domain Specific Accelerators** and hardware in general, along with the software support around them, such as compilers and simulators.

I am currently a visiting scholar at the **Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS)** in Saarbrücken. Earlier I interned at **NVIDIA** on formal verification, and I was a **Google Summer of Code 2025** contributor with the Metro-MPI++ project. I am a co-author of papers at DAC'25 and AIHWS'25 (the latter received a Best Paper Award).
